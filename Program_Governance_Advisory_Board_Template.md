Village Strong Advisory Council — Governance & Advisory Board Template

Purpose

Provide strategic guidance, ethical oversight, and program improvement recommendations for Village Strong.

Suggested Board Composition

Education Advisor
Experienced educator or school administrator.

Youth Development Specialist
Expert in mentoring or youth leadership programs.

Behavioral Health Advisor
Professional experienced in social-emotional development or trauma-informed practice.

Community Representative
Leader from a local organization or youth-serving agency.

Research Advisor
Supports evaluation design and data interpretation.

Responsibilities

- Review program outcomes
- Advise on program expansion
- Strengthen partnerships
- Ensure youth safety and ethical standards
- Support grant and funding opportunities

Governance Notes

- Board term length recommendations (e.g., 2–3 years)
- Meeting cadence (quarterly recommended)
- Conflict-of-interest disclosures
- Advisory vs. fiduciary roles (if incorporated separately)
