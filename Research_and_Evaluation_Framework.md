Research & Evaluation Framework — Village Strong

Research Objective

Evaluate the effectiveness of the Hero’s Path mentorship program in supporting youth leadership development, emotional resilience, and teamwork.

Key Research Questions

1. Does participation in the Hero’s Path program improve youth leadership behaviors?
2. Does the program improve emotional regulation and conflict resolution skills?
3. Does participation increase confidence, perseverance, and teamwork?
4. How do mentorship interactions influence participant engagement and growth?

Outcome Areas to Measure

- Leadership development
- Emotional regulation
- Decision-making skills
- Teamwork and communication
- Resilience and perseverance

Data Collection Methods

- Participant reflection journals
- Mentor observation reports
- Pre- and post-program surveys
- Participation and completion tracking
- Qualitative interviews or focus groups

Reporting

Evaluation reports may include:

- Program completion rates
- Changes in leadership indicators
- Participant testimonials
- Mentor feedback
- Program improvement recommendations

Use cases for reports:

- grant applications
- school partnerships
- program replication
- academic publications
