Gamification Rules — Village Strong (Pilot)

Overview
Village Strength Points (VSP) are awarded for key behaviors. XP thresholds map to ranks.

Rank thresholds (configurable):
- Pathfinder: 0–199 VSP
- Guardian: 200–499 VSP
- Builder: 500–999 VSP
- Leader: 1000–1999 VSP
- Champion: 2000+ VSP

Point sources (examples):
- Module completion: 50 VSP
- Reflection submitted: 20 VSP
- Thoughtful mentor-rated reflection: +10–30 VSP (mentor awards)
- Forum participation (meaningful post): 5–15 VSP
- Team challenge contribution: 40 VSP
- Helping a teammate (mentor-validated): 25 VSP
- Streak bonus (3+ weeks consecutive): 30 VSP

Badges
- First Step (earn on Module 1 completion)
- Week 3 Complete
- Midway Hero (Module 5 complete)
- Final Trial Complete
- Courage, Wisdom, Honor, Strength, Unity (award via rubric/mentor recommendation)
- Team Builder, Conflict Resolver, Encourager, Role Model, Village Champion

Leaderboard
- Leaderboards show cohort-level and site-level rankings; configurable to protect privacy (opt-in).

Manual vs Automatic awards
- Automatic: badges and rank changes based on completion rules and XP ledger.
- Manual: mentor commendations and character badges awarded via mentor observation form.

Points ledger design
- Each entry: timestamp, user_id, source, points, granted_by (system/mentor), reason/comment.

Notes
- Keep thresholds conservative for pilot; monitor distribution and tune after first cohort.
- Ensure mentor-awarded points are logged with notes for research traceability.
