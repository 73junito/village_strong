# Ethics/IRB determination checklist

**Purpose:** Prepare a complete submission and preserve the reviewing body's
written determination. This checklist does not decide whether a project is
human-subjects research, exempt, or eligible for expedited review.

## Project intake

- [ ] Study title, code, protocol version, and accountable investigator
- [ ] Research questions and intended generalizable knowledge
- [ ] Instrument and version (FRI, PPARS, EFAS, or other)
- [ ] Development stage and analysis plan
- [ ] Recruitment population, locations, and jurisdictions
- [ ] Inclusion/exclusion criteria and sample-size rationale
- [ ] Recruitment materials and compensation
- [ ] Every participant interaction and data source
- [ ] Risks, safeguards, benefits, conflicts, funding, and collaborators
- [ ] Consent, withdrawal, privacy, retention, and incident plans
- [ ] Data-flow diagram and access-role matrix

## Determination questions for the reviewer

- [ ] Does the activity constitute research involving human subjects?
- [ ] Which review pathway and continuing oversight apply?
- [ ] May recruitment begin, and under which approved materials/version?
- [ ] Is documentation of consent required, altered, or waived?
- [ ] How must withdrawal and previously collected data be handled?
- [ ] Are additional privacy, breach, or jurisdictional requirements applicable?
- [ ] Are compensation and recruitment methods acceptable?
- [ ] Are recordings, sensitive questions, or third-party data approved?
- [ ] Is the proposed retention schedule acceptable?
- [ ] What events, deviations, complaints, or changes require reporting?

## Additional gate for anyone under 18

- [ ] Written determination covers inclusion of minors
- [ ] Parental/guardian permission requirements are documented
- [ ] Age-appropriate assent requirements are documented
- [ ] Age verification and re-consent at adulthood are addressed
- [ ] Privacy from parents/guardians and limits to confidentiality are addressed
- [ ] Mandatory-reporting and distress escalation procedures are approved
- [ ] Recruitment cannot directly target minors before all approvals are final

## Change control

Recruitment and collection remain prohibited until the decision is documented.
After approval, submit protocol, consent, population, recruitment, data,
instrument, purpose, vendor, and security changes for review before adoption,
except immediate actions required to remove an apparent hazard. Document every
deviation and closeout according to the reviewing body's instructions.
