## Invitation and purpose

You are invited to consider a research study about [plain-language purpose].
Taking part is voluntary. The proposed FRI, PPARS, and EFAS measures are under
development and are not diagnostic or clinical instruments.

## What participation involves

Explain screening, questions or interviews, approximate time, number of
sessions, recordings (if any), follow-up, and any activities that may be
skipped. Do not add an activity that is absent from the approved protocol.

## Risks or discomforts

Describe reasonably foreseeable risks in plain language, including emotional
discomfort, privacy loss, and limits to confidentiality. State how participants
may pause, skip a question, or stop.

## Benefits, alternatives, costs, and compensation

State whether there is any direct benefit; do not promise one. Explain
alternatives to participation, costs, compensation, and prorating after early
withdrawal.

## Privacy and data use

Explain:

- what data will be collected and why;
- that the research database uses a participant code rather than direct
  identifiers;
- where any separately approved identity-linkage information is kept;
- who may access each record class;
- retention and destruction periods;
- whether de-identified data may be shared or published;
- limits to confidentiality and required disclosures, if applicable.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. Refusal or withdrawal will not affect access to
public Village Strong or FAMILY information or any service to which the person
is otherwise entitled.

State exactly how withdrawal is requested and what happens to data already
collected. The choice must match the approved protocol; do not promise deletion
if the approved study requires lawful retention, and do not imply indefinite
retention without a documented basis.

## Questions, concerns, injuries, and participant rights

Provide separate contacts for study questions, participant-rights questions,
privacy/security concerns, and research-related injury if applicable.

## Consent statement

Use only the signature or electronic-consent language approved for this study.
The research schema records the consent fact, scope, version, and time; it must
not store a signature image, identity document, or raw consent file.

## Additional protections

This adult template is not sufficient for children or adolescents. Parental
permission and age-appropriate assent materials require separate review and
written approval before any minor is approached.
