Village Strong Foundation

The Hero’s Path Mentorship Program — Moodle Course Shell Blueprint

Program Developer & Research Lead: Daniel A. Rodriguez
Software Developer & Technical Systems Architect: Rafael Rodriguez Jr.

1. Course Overview

Course Name: The Hero’s Path Leadership Journey
Course Length: 10 Weeks
Target Participants: Youth ages 12–18 (adaptable)
Course Format: Weekly mentorship sessions combining narrative challenges, guided reflection, and teamwork activities.
Learning Environment: Mentor-facilitated group learning supported by digital resources and reflection tools.

2. Course Navigation Structure

Module 0 – Welcome to the Village
Module 1 – The Call to Adventure
Module 2 – Character Creation
Module 3 – The Storm of Anger
Module 4 – The Broken Bridge
Module 5 – The Mountain of Doubt
Module 6 – The Shadow of Temptation
Module 7 – Leadership in Action
Module 8 – Preparing for the Final Trial
Module 9 – The Final Trial
Module 10 – Champion Ceremony

3. Standard Weekly Lesson Layout

Section 1 — Opening Reflection
- Short video or reading
- Reflection question
- Mentor discussion prompt

Section 2 — Story Scenario
- H5P interactive story or narrative text
- Mentor storytelling options

Section 3 — Team Discussion
- Moodle forum or live discussion

Section 4 — Decision Challenge
- Multiple-choice scenario, branching narrative, or role-play submission

Section 5 — Leadership Lesson
- Short mentor-led explanation; downloadable guide

Section 6 — Reflection Journal
- Private assignment; mentor review

Section 7 — Village Strength Points
- Points for activity completion, teamwork, reflections, leadership

4. Rank Progression System

Ranks: Pathfinder → Guardian → Builder → Leader → Champion
Badges awarded at point milestones; XP thresholds configurable in gamification plugin.

5. Mentor Tools

Mentor Dashboard: roster, participation tracker, reflection review, mentor observation form.
Mentor Observation Form: teamwork, leadership behavior, emotional regulation, encouragement.

6. Participant Dashboard

Shows current rank, points, completed modules, upcoming challenges, mentor feedback, progress bars.

7. Program Completion Requirements

- Complete all modules
- Participate in discussions
- Submit reflections
- Complete final leadership challenge

Rewards: Village Champion Certificate

8. Final Ceremony Module

Reflection, recognition, mentor commendations, certificate award, participant sharing.

9. Data & Evaluation Integration

Collect: pre/post surveys, mentor observation scores, participation metrics, reflection themes.

10. Technical Implementation Notes

Recommended Moodle features: completion tracking, custom badges, H5P, feedback, reflection assignments, gamification plugin.
Course template should be clonable for partner deployment.

11. Future Expansion

Hero’s Path Level 2, Mentor Certification Academy, School Cohorts, Community Camps, National Network.
